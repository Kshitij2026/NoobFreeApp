Safety/platform constraints:
- These actions invoke Android's own system UI. They do not bypass Device Admin, Device Owner, Profile Owner, uninstall restrictions, or organization policy.
- If Android Device Policy is Device Owner, Android will block ordinary uninstall. Authorized unenrollment is required.
- A normal app cannot remove another app's Device Owner/Profile Owner status or silently deactivate its admin component.
